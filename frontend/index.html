Built around bidirectional peer matching

Instead of one-way tutoring boards, SkillSwap compares what you can teach against what another student wants to learn—and verifies the reverse.

01. Mutual Skill Matching

Example: How Ahmed and Sara connect

When Ahmed lists Python Programming under Teach and Graphic Design under Learn, and Sara lists the exact inverse, SkillSwap calculates their compatibility and opens a direct exchange proposal.

02. Structured Workflows

From request to completed review

Every exchange follows clear milestones so both students stay aligned on timing, agenda, and learning outcomes:

  1. Send a targeted exchange request with offered & requested skills.
  2. Accept or decline incoming proposals from your inbox.
  3. Schedule an Online or In-person session date and agenda.
  4. Mark the session completed to unlock verified 5-star reviews.
